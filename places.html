01. Van Vihar National Park

If you are looking for peaceful greenery and wildlife within city limits, Van Vihar is one of the best places to visit in Bhopal with friends. The park sits alongside Upper Lake and covers over 11 square kilometres. You can walk, cycle, or ride e-vehicles through its tree-lined paths while spotting animals in natural enclosures. The park is home to Bengal tigers, lions, leopards, and sloth bears, along with freely roaming deer, nilgai, and blackbucks. It is a good break from the noise of the city and lets you spend a few quiet hours with nature. Birdwatching is best done in the early morning, and larger animals near the lake are more likely to be seen at sunset.

Location: 69J8+VHM, Lake View Walk Path, Krishna Nagar, Shymala Hills, Bhopal
Timings: 06:30 a.m. to 06:30 p.m. (Fridays closed)

02. Upper Lake

Upper Lake, also known as Bhojtal, is among the best places to visit in Bhopal with friends. It is one of the city’s largest man-made lakes and plays an important role in supplying drinking water. There are walking paths, food stands, and paddle boats all around the lake, so it's a great place to hang out with friends, take pictures, or just relax by the water. You can have fun on the water in a variety of ways, including paddle boats and speedboats. The Raja Bhoj statue beside the lake is illuminated at night, which adds to the area's attractiveness. On weekends, people come here to unwind, creating a bustling yet tranquil atmosphere.

Location: 69VJ+G89, Boat Club, Upper Lake, Upper Lake Area, Bhopal
Timings: 06:00 a.m. to 07:00 p.m.

03. Moti Masjid

One of the oldest landmarks and places to see in Bhopal is Moti Masjid. Constructed by Sikandar Begum in 1860, it is notable for its Mughal-style architecture and has historical and religious significance. It is conveniently placed in the centre of the old city, making it a great place to stop when exploring the local markets or historical sites. You will notice its white marble domes and two red sandstone minarets with golden tops. The mosque is compact, neat, and still actively used by devotees. The courtyard is open to visitors during non-prayer times. If you are interested in photography or architecture, visiting around sunset gives you great light for capturing the details.

Location: 794X+6J7 Moti Masjid, Hawamahal Rd, Chowki Talaiya, Bhopal
Timings: 06:00 a.m. to 07:00 p.m.

04. Birla Mandir (Lakshmi Narayan Temple)

Birla Mandir is one of the best places to visit in Bhopal for peaceful surroundings and introspection. The temple, constructed out of marble commemorates Lord Vishnu and Goddess Lakshmi and is located atop Arera Hills. It has a simple, clean style with carved pillars and a well-kept garden surrounding it. Birla Mandir is also regarded as one of the notable temples of Madhya Pradesh. From the top, you will get a good view of Bhopal city and Upper Lake. The temple is usually not too crowded, so you can sit quietly or walk around at ease. Visitors often combine it with a visit to the nearby Birla Museum. It is a good stop if you want a quiet break during your trip to Bhopal.

Location: Ke Samne, Birla Mandir Rd, Arera Hills, Bhopal
Timings: 04:00 a.m. to 09:00 p.m.

05. Lower Lake

Lower Lake, or Chhota Talaab, is one of the peaceful places to visit in Bhopal with family. It lies next to Upper Lake and adds to the natural setting of the city centre. It is a smaller water body but still supports local birdlife and serves as a quiet place for morning or evening walks. There are boating facilities on the lake, and the pathways are suitable for strolling with children or older visitors. You will often see migratory birds near the banks during winter months. If you are exploring the old city, Lower Lake is a good spot to pause, relax, and enjoy views of Bhopal’s skyline. The lake is bathed in an aura of blissful quiet and tranquillity, making it perfect for nature lovers and tourists alike it is one of the most picturesque places in Bhopal.

Location: Jahangirabad, Bhopal
Timings: 09:00 a.m. to 05:00 p.m.

06. Birla Museum

For those who are interested in history, archaeology, or sculpture, the Birla Museum is among the best places to visit in Bhopal. It is located in Arera Hills and is close to the Birla Mandir. The museum was established in 1971 and houses prehistoric tools, tribal artefacts, and stone carvings from across Madhya Pradesh. As a visitor, you will see rare idols of Lord Vishnu, Shiva, and Chamunda, along with Neolithic and Chalcolithic tools. There are also exhibits showing early human settlements in Central India. The museum is well maintained and does not take more than an hour to explore. The campus also offers a good view of the city from above.

Location: 6CQ5+477, Arera Hills, Bhopal
Timings: 09:30 a.m. to 07:00 p.m.

07. Taj ul Masjid

Taj-Ul-Masjid is one of the largest mosques in India and a major highlight among places to visit in Bhopal. It was started during Nawab Shah Jahan Begum’s rule and completed much later. The building has three pink sandstone domes, a large courtyard, and tall minarets. You can explore the open area, see the water tank used for ablution, and look at the fine detailing on its walls. The mosque is open to tourists except during prayer times. Because of the weekly prayers, it is busiest on Fridays. For a better experience, arrive early in the morning or right before sunset, and dress with respect.

Location: NH 12, Kohefiza, Bhopal
Timings: Open 24 hours.

08. Bhojpur Shiva Temple

Bhojpur Shiva Temple is one of the most fascinating places to see in Bhopal if you are interested in religious heritage. Located about 28 kilometres from Bhopal, this 11th-century temple was built by Raja Bhoj and is famous for housing one of the tallest Shiva Lingams in India. The lingam was carved from a single rock and stands about 7.5 feet tall. The temple is a unique example of an incomplete historical construction because it was constructed of sandstone and contains unfinished portions. The temple, neighbouring ruins, and the historic dam constructed over the Betwa River are open for exploration by tourists. Location: Bhojpur Temple, Bhojpur Rd, Bhojpur, Madhya Pradesh

Timings: 06:00 a.m. to 06:00 p.m.

09. Tribal Museum, Bhopal

Located on Shyamla Hills Road, the Tribal Museum is one of the most unique places to visit in Bhopal with family. It is run by the Madhya Pradesh Tribal Welfare Department and offers a creative representation of the lives of the Gond, Bhil, Baiga, Korku, Sahariya, and Kol tribes of the state. Inside the museum, you can see realistic models of tribal homes, wedding rituals, musical performances, and hunting scenes. The displays are colourful, fun for kids, and nice to look at. The "Chinhari" section has native tribal arts and crafts that you shouldn't miss. There is also a small amphitheatre for concerts and a gift shop with handmade items inside the museum.

Location: 69PP+52, Shyamla Hills Rd, near State Museum, Shymala Hills, Bhopal
Timings: 12:00 p.m. to 08:00 p.m. (Mondays closed)

10. Manuabhan Tekri

Manuabhan Tekri is among the more tranquil places to visit in Bhopal, offering panoramic vistas, short hikes, and local history. Located atop Lalghati Hill, this site provides a magnificent view of the city and is famous for its Jaintemple and ropeway. You can visit the Manuabhan Jain Temple, which attracts both tourists and devotees. The ropeway ride is short but gives a nice aerial view. The area is ideal for morning walks, and locals often visit here for relaxation. There are benches, shaded spots, and small vendors near the parking area. It is best visited in the morning or during sunset hours.

Location: 79H7+M2V, Lalghati Rd, Tilak Nagar, Lalghati, Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh
Timings: 06:30 a.m. to 08:00 p.m.
